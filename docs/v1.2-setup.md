# v1.2: setup checklist

What to do after deploying v1.2. It follows on from `v1.1-setup.md`.

## 1. Family members (Family → Family Members)
The reading log and other child-only features need to know who the children are:
- **Tick "Parent"** for each grown-up.
- **Set a year group** (for example "Year 4") for each child. The School inbox uses it too.

Only people who are **not** marked Parent **and** have a year group get a "Read tonight" tick.
Until someone qualifies, the Homework widget shows no reading row. Admin's Reading Log then says
"Set a year group for each child in Family Members to start the reading log."

## 2. Homework subjects (Family → Homework)
- Each homework item gets a subject icon and colour: Maths, English, Reading, Science, Topic or
  Other.
- It's worked out from the subject you type ("Spellings" is English, "TTRS" is Maths). Use the
  **Icon** menu to pick one yourself. School inbox items get theirs from the subject Claude reads.
- Existing homework was sorted into subjects when you updated.

## 3. Reading log and done history
- Children tick **Read tonight** on the Homework widget, any day, including weekends and holidays.
- **Family → Reading Log** shows the last 4 weeks per child, to help with the school reading
  record. Days off school are shaded. There are no points.
- **Family → Homework → Recently ticked** lists when homework was ticked done or unticked on the
  wall.
